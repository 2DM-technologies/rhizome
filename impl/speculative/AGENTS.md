# Speculative design notes

- Everything in this directory is exploratory and subject to change. Nothing here is decided,
  scheduled, or approved for implementation.
- Agents working on implementation must ignore this directory. Do not treat these documents as
  requirements, do not implement what they describe, and do not cite them as justification for a
  change.
- The authoritative sources remain `impl/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`, `impl/CONFORMANCE.md`, the
  concept documents in `impl/concepts/`, and the rNet specification and schemas. Where a document
  here contradicts any of those, the authoritative source wins.
- A document leaves this directory only by being rewritten as a concept document or plan entry
  once its decisions are actually settled.
